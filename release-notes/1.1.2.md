# Velocity 1.1.2

- Adds high-accuracy fused location alongside GPS to improve location acquisition on phones with Google Play services, including Samsung devices.
- Keeps GPS recording available on devices without Google Play services.
- Shows when a location has been received but needs better accuracy, instead of silently waiting for a GPS fix.
- Adds Retry GPS without resetting your current journey or connecting missing route sections.
- Correctly checks the device's location setting. Stale and inaccurate positions remain excluded from speed and distance.
