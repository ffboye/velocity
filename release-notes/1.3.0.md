# Velocity 1.3.0

- Replaces decorative gradients and glows with a minimal, consistent interface and clearer controls.
- Adds editable journey names and notes when saving and reviewing trips. Existing journeys retain their routes and date/time labels.
- Records speed-over-time graphs for new journeys, preserving manual pauses and reception gaps. Older journeys are clearly identified when graph data is unavailable.
- Adds distance, recorded-time, weighted average-speed, top-speed and seven-day distance insights. Recorded time includes stationary recording time and excludes manual pauses.
- Adds real street maps with locally drawn routes, start/finish markers and separate route sections across GPS gaps.
- Adds optional offline map areas, estimated download size, storage checks, Wi-Fi-only downloads, pause/resume and deletion. Downloads default off and pause in the background.
- Adds a Velocity tracking switch. Turning it off pauses an active journey and stops location requests; turning it on requires a manual Resume.
- Adds clear Good, Weak, Searching and Off GPS states based on location accuracy and freshness, separately from satellite reception.
- Retains local backups, GPX export, background recording, large-text support, accessible controls and signed in-app updates.
