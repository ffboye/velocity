# Velocity 1.4.0

- Refines the interface with consistent typography, calm blue accents, lighter card outlines and a simpler speedometer.
- Adds short tab transitions, matching controls across screens, and full-width tappable preference rows.
- Groups display settings together and keeps privacy details available on demand.
- Gives journey details the full screen and adds a guide line to selected speed-graph readings.
- Keeps GPS controls, the optional GPS tab, route maps, offline download choices and saved journeys.
