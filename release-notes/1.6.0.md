A bolder speedometer with a gradient arc, one GPS status pill, and glanceable altitude and session-peak stats. Your configured speed reminder adds a badge and warning ring only while enabled, with amber and red feedback above your chosen limit. It is a personal reminder, not automatic road-speed-limit data.

Journey history now uses a balanced summary grid and clean previews of each recorded route. Journey details open with the speed graph. Add Commute, Cycling, Drive or Walk tags when saving or editing a journey; tags are retained in journey backups and GPX exports.

Settings are grouped into Behavior, Appearance and Diagnostics. Easy reading is now Large text mode, with the existing preference retained. High-contrast daylight mode applies throughout the app, including dialogs and maps controls. Switches have clear filled ON states.

Choose tap haptics and trip-action haptics separately. Optional distance chimes sound at each kilometre or mile, according to your selected units and notification volume. Chimes default to off and do not replay old milestones when enabled, resumed or switched between units.
