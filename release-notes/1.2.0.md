# Velocity 1.2.0

- Adds a GPS connectivity tab with live satellite counts, satellites used in the receiver's fix, signal strength bars and a north-up sky view.
- Shows location accuracy, age, provider and time to first satellite fix, with clear permission, disabled-location and unavailable-data states.
- Unifies colours, headers, cards and location status across Speedometer, Journeys, GPS and Settings.
- Keeps pause, resume and finish controls available across all tabs while recording.
- Reconnects after device location is switched off and on, without resetting the journey or joining gaps in the route.
- Adds persistent save-error feedback and improves journey detail text contrast and back navigation.
- Waits for pending journey saves before handing off an update, and blocks installation until failed saves are retried successfully.
